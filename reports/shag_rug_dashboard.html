Mitigation: Opt for neutral or timeless color palettes (e.g., beige, gray, or cream) to extend relevance. Invest in high-quality, classic designs that transcend fleeting trends. Consider modular or reversible shag rugs that allow for design flexibility.

high

Use performance-treated fibers (e.g., stain-resistant polyester) to reduce maintenance burdens. Clearly label care instructions and provide video tutorials.

Performance Risks

high

Durability is a critical concern for shag rugs, particularly in high-traffic areas. Polyester fibers (a common material) are prone to crushing, matting, and pilling over time. Low-quality construction

Mitigation: Use high-density, tightly twisted fibers to improve resilience. Reinforce edges with serging or binding to prevent fraying. Offer extended warranties for premium lines. Provide clear guidance on ideal placement (e.g., low-traffic areas like bedrooms).
